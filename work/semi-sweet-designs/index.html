A cookie brand, rolled out for decorators.

A one-stop shop for recipes, cutters, tutorials, packaging, and apparel. Teaching people to decorate through unique tools and tutorials online.

Role
Co-OwnerBrand Designer
Scope
Brand IdentityProduct DesignIllustrationMarketing
Timeline

Challenge

Cookie decorating can feel intimidating, and decorators need tools, instruction, and packaging from several places.

Solution

One friendly brand that connects every step, from the first cutter to the finished, gift-ready cookie.

Outcome

A loyal community of decorators, with 45.5K customers and 41.1% coming back to buy again.

  • 1,104Cutter Designs
  • 165.6KOrders Fulfilled
  • 47.8KEmail Subscribers
  • 356.8KSocial Followers

The Approach

Making impressive designs easier to create.

We believed that great design should make creativity more accessible. By simplifying shapes, removing unnecessary complexity, and providing helpful decorating tools and tutorials, we made it easier for bakers of all skill levels to achieve professional-looking results.

Strategy Meets Creative

Creative Direction & Brand Leadership

Beyond designing the brand, I led the creative direction and marketing for Semi Sweet Designs. I planned seasonal campaigns, prioritized product launches, and used customer insights to guide creative decisions and improve the shopping experience.

  • Campaign Planning

    Planned seasonal collections and coordinated product launches across email, social, and the storefront.

  • Brand & Production

    Maintained a consistent visual identity across products, packaging, apparel, and marketing assets, from concept to production.

  • Performance & Iteration

    Used sales trends, customer behavior, and campaign performance to refine product presentation, prioritize creative work, and improve conversion.

Brand identity

A name that’s sweet, but not too serious.

The name Semi Sweet Designs captures our playful approach to cookie decorating. We brought that personality to life through icing-inspired lettering, cheerful colors, and a visual identity designed to feel welcoming and approachable.

A personality you can taste.

Product Design

Thoughtfully designed, from concept to cookie.

Every product began with one goal: make cookie decorating approachable, enjoyable, and rewarding. Here’s how one Frankenstein cutter came to life.

  1. 1Concept

    Simple by design.

    Simplified shapes that keep their personality and fit our packaging.

  2. 2Prototype

    Ideas made tangible.

    3D modeled and printed in-house, then tested for durability and clean cuts.

  3. 3Tutorials

    Creativity made accessible.

    Free tutorials, videos, and projector templates for every skill level.

  4. 4Packaging

    The perfect finishing touch.

    Coordinating backers, gift tags, and bows that make gifting easy.
