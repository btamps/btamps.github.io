Turning complex technology into visual stories.

As a marketing designer for Cisco Meraki, I helped translate complex products and big ideas into clear, engaging visual campaigns across digital, events, launches, and brand.

The brief

Create clear, friendly technology communications that make Meraki’s cloud-managed solutions easy to understand and relevant for public-sector audiences.

My approach

Use an approachable illustration style and flexible design system that simplifies complex ideas, stays consistent across formats, and brings the brand to life.

The work

A cohesive set of marketing campaigns, ebooks, emails, and internal brand communications that make Meraki’s technology feel more approachable, visual, and distinctly Cisco Meraki.

Digital Campaigns - Smart Communities

Making connected communities easier to picture

For Meraki’s Smart Communities campaign, I designed a series of digital ads that visualized how connected technology fits into everyday environments. I used illustration to bring the concept to life, then adapted the designs across multiple banner sizes while keeping the message and visual style consistent.

Editorial Design & Illustration

Turning technical content into an engaging read

I designed and illustrated an ebook about network security in education, turning technical content into a more approachable visual experience. Custom chapter illustrations helped introduce key concepts, while a consistent layout and typography made the information easier to navigate and digest.

Email Campaigns

Illustrating complex concepts for email

I designed and illustrated a series of marketing emails that made complex networking concepts easier to understand. Each email paired a custom header illustration with a clear, scannable layout, helping readers quickly grasp the main idea and explore the technology behind it.

Internal branding and creative

Bringing the brand into everyday experiences

Beyond customer-facing campaigns, I created illustrations, posters, logos, and merchandise for internal initiatives. These projects gave me opportunities to explore different creative directions while keeping the work connected to Meraki’s brand.
