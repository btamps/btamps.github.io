Turning complex technology into visual stories.

As a marketing designer for Cisco Meraki, I helped translate complex products and big ideas into clear, engaging visual campaigns across digital, events, launches, and brand.

The brief

Create clear, friendly technology communications that make Meraki’s cloud-managed solutions easy to understand and relevant for public-sector audiences.

My approach

Use an approachable illustration style and flexible design system that simplifies complex ideas, stays consistent across formats, and brings the brand to life.

The work

A cohesive set of marketing campaigns, ebooks, emails, and internal brand communications that make Meraki’s technology feel more approachable, visual, and distinctly Cisco Meraki.

Digital Campaigns

Campaigns that connect.

A digital advertising campaign promoting Meraki’s cloud-managed technology for public-sector organizations. I developed a flexible illustration system for smarter, more connected communities.

Editorial Design & Illustration

Complex ideas, clearly illustrated.

For Meraki’s End-to-End Security in Education ebook, I combined editorial design with custom illustrations to make a technical topic feel approachable. Familiar school settings connect abstract security concepts to the classrooms, devices, and campus spaces they protect.

I designed the cover and interior layouts around a consistent visual system: clear typographic hierarchy, illustrated chapter openers, and icon-led sidebars that highlight key takeaways. Meraki’s green palette ties the publication together, creating a cohesive reading experience from cover to conclusion.
