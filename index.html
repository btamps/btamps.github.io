I’m Billy, a brand and visual designer based in Tampa, Florida. I combine design and illustration to help technology companies explain complex ideas and connect with people.

My background includes marketing design at Cisco Meraki, brand illustration for Highfive, and co-founding Semi Sweet Designs. Building a business has shaped how I work: understand the audience, make thoughtful creative decisions, and see the details through.

I’m looking to join a tech team where I can bring that approach to brand campaigns, digital experiences, and illustration—from the first sketch to the final launch.

Let’s make something people remember.

Looking for a designer to bring clarity, personality, and fresh ideas to your brand? I’d love to join your team.
